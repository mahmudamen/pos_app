مميزات النظام
Farm Management Module Features
📍
Hierarchical Locations
مواقع هرمية
4-level hierarchy: Farm → Sector → Unite → House. Each level maps to analytic accounts. Assign workers and costs per location. Kanban view for visual management.
🌱
Crop Management
إدارة المحاصيل
Track crop varieties, planting dates, growth stages, expected yields. Support for sugarcane, dates, vegetables, cotton, wheat. Crop rotation planning across seasons.
📋
Agriculture Work Orders
أوامر العمل الزراعية
Modeled after mrp.production. Create orders for planting, irrigation, fertilizing, spraying, harvesting. Track labor hours, materials used, equipment allocated per order.
📦
Harvest & Inventory
الحصاد والمخزون
Stock movement wizards auto-create warehouse receipts on harvest. Track produce from field to storage to sale. Lot tracking for quality control. Waste recording.
💰
Cost Analytics
تحليل التكاليف
Real P&L per farm, per crop, per season. Track all inputs: seeds, fertilizer, water, labor, equipment. Compare expected vs actual costs. Seasonal trends dashboard.
👨‍🌾
Worker Payroll
رواتب العمال
Assign workers to locations. Track attendance via work orders. Auto-calculate wages (daily, task-based, seasonal). Integrates with Odoo HR and Payroll modules.

🏆 Real Deployment — Client: Qatf Company
Agriculture ERP in Production
Built and deployed for real farming operations. Not a demo — a working system.
المشكلة
Qatf Company manages multiple farm locations with different crops. Manual paper records made it impossible to track costs per location, manage worker assignments, or plan seasonal rotations. No visibility into which crops were profitable.
الحل
We built farm_management for Odoo 17: 4-level farm hierarchy, crop.crop model for lifecycle tracking, work orders for all farming operations, stock wizards for harvest, analytic accounts per location, payroll integration for seasonal workers.
النتيجة
Complete visibility into per-crop profitability. Worker payroll automated (saved 2 days/month). Harvest tracking eliminated discrepancies. Management now plans seasons based on data, not guesswork. Cost reduction estimated at 25%.
