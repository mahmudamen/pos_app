📡 Network Copilot · Android 10+ · Offline-First
Diagnose Like a Network Engineer — Right From Your Phone
Meet Network Copilot — a professional network diagnostics and operations toolkit that runs offline-first on your device. 40+ tools, an explainable diagnostic engine with a health score, a no-root app firewall, and an AI Network Analyst across 8 providers. Built by XamlTech with a dark glass UI and full Arabic/English (RTL) support.

📋 Privacy Policy
🛠️40+Diagnostic Tools
🤖8AI Providers
🧪318Tests Passing
🌐AR/ENBilingual · RTL · Offline
🏠
Command-Center Dashboard
Live connectivity (Wi-Fi, cellular, VPN, Ethernet, metered), interface details — IPv4/IPv6, gateway, MTU — quick-launch tool tiles and an explainable weighted health score.
⚡
Core Diagnostics
Ping with jitter & loss, DNS lookups (A/AAAA/MX/NS/TXT/CNAME + PTR), an HTTP/HTTPS builder with DNS→TLS→first-byte timing breakdown, speed test and TCP port scanner.
📶
WiFi & Signal Analyzer
Live band, link speed, BSSID and security, nearby network scans, and a precise room-by-room signal mapper.
🌍
LAN, IP & Protocol Lab
Subnet / IPv4 CIDR and IPv6 calculators, advanced IPv6 analysis (multicast scope, embedded v4, NAT64), mDNS/UPnP discovery, Wake-on-LAN and SNMP walks.
🔮
Intelligent Engine
Rule-based diagnostics on an Evidence → Inference → Action model with confidence levels, custom JSON rules, and professional reports (Markdown/JSON/HTML, 15 sections).
🔐
Privacy by Default
Masked IPs, MACs and SSIDs with one-tap reveal, encrypted on-device key storage (AES256-GCM), no cloud required, and reports you fully control.
📡
Monitor & Control
Live system monitor with a foreground-service notification and home-screen widget, Quick Settings tiles (Scan, Monitor, Speed, Port, Firewall) and in-app notification history.
🔐
App Firewall (No-Root)
A local VPN-based firewall that blocks selected apps entirely by dropping their traffic on-device — no byte ever leaves your phone.
👤
Accounts & Sync
Google Sign-In, history synced under your own user scope, projects shared with teams — while anonymous, fully offline use needs no account.
🤖 Built-in AI
Network Copilot
The AI Network Analyst turns raw scan results into plain-language answers, root-cause analysis and professional reports — your keys, stored encrypted, with diagnostic context sanitized before it leaves your phone. On-device analysis is free; unlimited in Premium.
🔎
Analyze
Break down a scan or result into a clear, structured diagnosis.
💡
Explain
Plain-language explanation of what each number and field means.
🩺
Troubleshoot
Step-by-step fix guidance based on the detected symptoms.
📄
Report
A complete diagnostic report exported as Markdown, JSON or HTML.
Bring your own model. Works with 8 providers — Gemini, Kimi, OpenAI, Claude, GitHub Copilot, Azure OpenAI, Meta Llama — or a local Ollama server on your LAN: no cloud, no cost, fully private. Free credits daily, ad-rewarded top-ups, or unlimited with Premium.
