Common Questions About AI Copilots
How do I choose between AI Marketing Copilot and AI POS Copilot?
+
AI Marketing Copilot optimizes how you acquire customers — campaign ROI, budget allocation, and lead scoring.
AI POS Copilot optimizes what happens at checkout — transaction value, cross-sell, and inventory clearance.
Deploy both for a unified AI strategy: Marketing brings customers in, POS increases their spend. Combined effect: 52% revenue growth.
Are these modules free?
+
Yes, both AI Marketing Copilot and AI POS Copilot are free to install from the Odoo Apps Store.
No licensing fees, no per-user charges. You pay for Odoo hosting/service, not for the modules themselves.
What about data privacy and security?
+
All data stays within your Odoo instance. No cloud APIs, no external processing, no third-party data storage.
The AI runs locally on your server. Fully GDPR and Egyptian data regulation compliant.
Can I integrate these with my existing tools?
+
Yes. Both modules provide Python APIs and JSON webhooks. You can send recommendations to
HubSpot, Klaviyo, email platforms, ad networks, CRMs, and more. Full documentation and examples are included.
Do I need machine learning expertise to use these?
+
No. Both copilots work out of the box with zero configuration. Install, load demo data, and start
getting AI-powered recommendations immediately. The ML models are pre-trained and continuously learn from your data.
