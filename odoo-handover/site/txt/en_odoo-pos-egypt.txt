🏆 Real Deployment
POS in Production: Al-Ghazl Al-Arabi
A textile trading company needed POS with fabric cutting management. Here's what we built.
The Problem
Al-Ghazl Al-Arabi sells fabric by the meter. Cashiers needed to create cutting orders (fabric type, dimensions, quantity) directly from the POS screen. Standard Odoo POS doesn't support this.
Our Solution
We built POS Cutting Orders (ma_pos_cutting) — a custom module that adds a cutting order workflow to the POS. Dedicated cutting screen, real-time tracking, manager PIN for approvals, dark/light mode. Now published on Odoo Apps Store.
Results
Multi-session POS across 2 locations. Real-time stock sync between stores. Manager security controls. Cutting orders processed 3x faster than paper-based system. Zero stock discrepancies.
POS Frequently Asked Questions
How much does Odoo POS cost in Egypt?
Odoo Community POS is free. Enterprise requires licensing. Our implementation packages start from basic single-store setup. Custom modules on Odoo Apps Store start at €99. Contact us for a detailed quote based on your needs.
Does Odoo POS work without internet?
Yes. Odoo POS is a Progressive Web App that continues working when internet drops. All transactions sync automatically when connection returns. We configure offline mode as part of every deployment.
What printers work with Odoo POS?
ESC/POS thermal printers: Epson TM-T20III, Star TSP143, and compatible models. Connected via Odoo IoT Box (USB/Bluetooth) or direct network printing (Ethernet/Wi-Fi). We handle printer procurement and setup.
Can I use Odoo POS for a restaurant?
Yes. Built-in floor plans, table management, kitchen display (KDS), split bills, tips. Kitchen printing sends food orders directly to the kitchen printer. Full integration with inventory for ingredient tracking.
How long does POS setup take?
Single store: 3-5 days. Multi-store: 1-2 weeks. With custom modules: 2-4 weeks. Includes product import, hardware setup, receipt configuration, staff training, and testing.
Do you serve areas outside Aswan?
Yes. Software setup is done remotely. For hardware installation, we travel to your location. We serve all of Egypt, with focus on Upper Egypt (Aswan, Luxor, Qena, Sohag).
