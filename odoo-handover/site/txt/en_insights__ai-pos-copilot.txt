How AI POS Copilot Works
Real-Time Processing Pipeline
When a cashier scans an item into the cart:
1️⃣ Cart Analysis
Analyze items in current cart, total value, customer category
2️⃣ Customer History
Check past purchases (what they usually buy together)
3️⃣ Product Matching
Find complementary products using category + tagging rules
4️⃣ Inventory Check
Only suggest in-stock items (prevents frustration)
5️⃣ Ranking by Relevance
Score candidates by margin, stock level, customer preference
6️⃣ Display
Show top 1-3 suggestions in Arabic/English in sidebar
Smart Algorithms Inside
Frequently Bought Together — If customers often buy A with B, suggest B when A is in cart
Sequential Patterns — Item X is usually bought before Y, so when X is added, surface Y
Price Elasticity — Don't suggest expensive items after cheap items (customer psychology)
Slow Stock Mover Detection — Suggest items that are overstocked to clear inventory
Customer Segmentation — Recommendations differ by customer type (wholesale vs. retail, new vs. repeat)
Real-Time Feedback — If a suggestion is rejected consistently, the system learns to stop suggesting it
